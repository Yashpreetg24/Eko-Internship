# Work from Home Policy

## Purpose
This document outlines the standard operating procedure for Work from Home Policy.

## Eligibility
All full-time employees are eligible for Work from Home Policy subject to departmental approval.

## Step-by-step process
1. Initiate the Work from Home Policy request via the employee portal.
2. Manager reviews and approves the request.
3. The relevant team processes the request.
4. Confirmation is sent to the employee.

## Expected timeline
The expected timeline for Work from Home Policy is 3-5 business days.

## Escalation contact
For any issues regarding Work from Home Policy, please contact the HR Helpdesk at hr@employeeclaw.com.
