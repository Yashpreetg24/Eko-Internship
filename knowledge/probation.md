# Probation

## Purpose
This document outlines the standard operating procedure for Probation.

## Eligibility
All full-time employees are eligible for Probation subject to departmental approval.

## Step-by-step process
1. Initiate the Probation request via the employee portal.
2. Manager reviews and approves the request.
3. The relevant team processes the request.
4. Confirmation is sent to the employee.

## Expected timeline
The expected timeline for Probation is 3-5 business days.

## Escalation contact
For any issues regarding Probation, please contact the HR Helpdesk at hr@employeeclaw.com.
