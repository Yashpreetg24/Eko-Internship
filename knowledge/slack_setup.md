# Slack Setup

## Purpose
This document outlines the standard operating procedure for Slack Setup.

## Eligibility
All full-time employees are eligible for Slack Setup subject to departmental approval.

## Step-by-step process
1. Initiate the Slack Setup request via the employee portal.
2. Manager reviews and approves the request.
3. The relevant team processes the request.
4. Confirmation is sent to the employee.

## Expected timeline
The expected timeline for Slack Setup is 3-5 business days.

## Escalation contact
For any issues regarding Slack Setup, please contact the HR Helpdesk at hr@employeeclaw.com.
