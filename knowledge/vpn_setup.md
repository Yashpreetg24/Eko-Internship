# VPN Setup

## Purpose
This document outlines the standard operating procedure for VPN Setup.

## Eligibility
All full-time employees are eligible for VPN Setup subject to departmental approval.

## Step-by-step process
1. Initiate the VPN Setup request via the employee portal.
2. Manager reviews and approves the request.
3. The relevant team processes the request.
4. Confirmation is sent to the employee.

## Expected timeline
The expected timeline for VPN Setup is 3-5 business days.

## Escalation contact
For any issues regarding VPN Setup, please contact the HR Helpdesk at hr@employeeclaw.com.
