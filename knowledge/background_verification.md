# Background Verification

## Purpose
This document outlines the standard operating procedure for Background Verification.

## Eligibility
All full-time employees are eligible for Background Verification subject to departmental approval.

## Step-by-step process
1. Initiate the Background Verification request via the employee portal.
2. Manager reviews and approves the request.
3. The relevant team processes the request.
4. Confirmation is sent to the employee.

## Expected timeline
The expected timeline for Background Verification is 3-5 business days.

## Escalation contact
For any issues regarding Background Verification, please contact the HR Helpdesk at hr@employeeclaw.com.
