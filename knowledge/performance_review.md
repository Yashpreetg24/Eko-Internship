# Performance Review

## Purpose
This document outlines the standard operating procedure for Performance Review.

## Eligibility
All full-time employees are eligible for Performance Review subject to departmental approval.

## Step-by-step process
1. Initiate the Performance Review request via the employee portal.
2. Manager reviews and approves the request.
3. The relevant team processes the request.
4. Confirmation is sent to the employee.

## Expected timeline
The expected timeline for Performance Review is 3-5 business days.

## Escalation contact
For any issues regarding Performance Review, please contact the HR Helpdesk at hr@employeeclaw.com.
