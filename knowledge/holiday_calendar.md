# Holiday Calendar

## Purpose
This document outlines the standard operating procedure for Holiday Calendar.

## Eligibility
All full-time employees are eligible for Holiday Calendar subject to departmental approval.

## Step-by-step process
1. Initiate the Holiday Calendar request via the employee portal.
2. Manager reviews and approves the request.
3. The relevant team processes the request.
4. Confirmation is sent to the employee.

## Expected timeline
The expected timeline for Holiday Calendar is 3-5 business days.

## Escalation contact
For any issues regarding Holiday Calendar, please contact the HR Helpdesk at hr@employeeclaw.com.
