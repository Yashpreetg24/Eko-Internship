# Insurance

## Purpose
This document outlines the standard operating procedure for Insurance.

## Eligibility
All full-time employees are eligible for Insurance subject to departmental approval.

## Step-by-step process
1. Initiate the Insurance request via the employee portal.
2. Manager reviews and approves the request.
3. The relevant team processes the request.
4. Confirmation is sent to the employee.

## Expected timeline
The expected timeline for Insurance is 3-5 business days.

## Escalation contact
For any issues regarding Insurance, please contact the HR Helpdesk at hr@employeeclaw.com.
