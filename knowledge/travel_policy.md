# Travel Policy

## Purpose
This document outlines the standard operating procedure for Travel Policy.

## Eligibility
All full-time employees are eligible for Travel Policy subject to departmental approval.

## Step-by-step process
1. Initiate the Travel Policy request via the employee portal.
2. Manager reviews and approves the request.
3. The relevant team processes the request.
4. Confirmation is sent to the employee.

## Expected timeline
The expected timeline for Travel Policy is 3-5 business days.

## Escalation contact
For any issues regarding Travel Policy, please contact the HR Helpdesk at hr@employeeclaw.com.
