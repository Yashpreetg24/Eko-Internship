# Company Email

## Purpose
This document outlines the standard operating procedure for Company Email.

## Eligibility
All full-time employees are eligible for Company Email subject to departmental approval.

## Step-by-step process
1. Initiate the Company Email request via the employee portal.
2. Manager reviews and approves the request.
3. The relevant team processes the request.
4. Confirmation is sent to the employee.

## Expected timeline
The expected timeline for Company Email is 3-5 business days.

## Escalation contact
For any issues regarding Company Email, please contact the HR Helpdesk at hr@employeeclaw.com.
