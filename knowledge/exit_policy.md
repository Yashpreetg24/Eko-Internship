# Exit Policy

## Purpose
This document outlines the standard operating procedure for Exit Policy.

## Eligibility
All full-time employees are eligible for Exit Policy subject to departmental approval.

## Step-by-step process
1. Initiate the Exit Policy request via the employee portal.
2. Manager reviews and approves the request.
3. The relevant team processes the request.
4. Confirmation is sent to the employee.

## Expected timeline
The expected timeline for Exit Policy is 3-5 business days.

## Escalation contact
For any issues regarding Exit Policy, please contact the HR Helpdesk at hr@employeeclaw.com.
