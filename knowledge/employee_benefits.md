# Employee Benefits

## Purpose
This document outlines the standard operating procedure for Employee Benefits.

## Eligibility
All full-time employees are eligible for Employee Benefits subject to departmental approval.

## Step-by-step process
1. Initiate the Employee Benefits request via the employee portal.
2. Manager reviews and approves the request.
3. The relevant team processes the request.
4. Confirmation is sent to the employee.

## Expected timeline
The expected timeline for Employee Benefits is 3-5 business days.

## Escalation contact
For any issues regarding Employee Benefits, please contact the HR Helpdesk at hr@employeeclaw.com.
