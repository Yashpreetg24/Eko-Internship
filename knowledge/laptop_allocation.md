# Laptop Allocation

## Purpose
This document outlines the standard operating procedure for Laptop Allocation.

## Eligibility
All full-time employees are eligible for Laptop Allocation subject to departmental approval.

## Step-by-step process
1. Initiate the Laptop Allocation request via the employee portal.
2. Manager reviews and approves the request.
3. The relevant team processes the request.
4. Confirmation is sent to the employee.

## Expected timeline
The expected timeline for Laptop Allocation is 3-5 business days.

## Escalation contact
For any issues regarding Laptop Allocation, please contact the HR Helpdesk at hr@employeeclaw.com.
