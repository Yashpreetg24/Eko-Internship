# Joining Process

## Purpose
This document outlines the standard operating procedure for Joining Process.

## Eligibility
All full-time employees are eligible for Joining Process subject to departmental approval.

## Step-by-step process
1. Initiate the Joining Process request via the employee portal.
2. Manager reviews and approves the request.
3. The relevant team processes the request.
4. Confirmation is sent to the employee.

## Expected timeline
The expected timeline for Joining Process is 3-5 business days.

## Escalation contact
For any issues regarding Joining Process, please contact the HR Helpdesk at hr@employeeclaw.com.
